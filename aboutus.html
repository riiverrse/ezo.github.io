About Us

Our Story

Ezo Kitchen & Sushi Bar is a new sushi and Asian fusion restaurant opening in the suburbs of Long Island, co-owned equally (50/50) by Michael Chen and Daniel Martinez. Both partners bring valuable expertise: Michael is Japanese-American (third generation, sansei) and trained under several master sushi chefs in Los Angeles and Tokyo before working in restaurant management. Daniel spent five years studying culinary traditions throughout Asia, including formal training in Japanese kaiseki cuisine in Kyoto and working in professional kitchens in Vietnam and Thailand. They met at a culinary institute and bonded over their shared passion for Asian cuisine.

Mission

Our mission is to provide an authentic and unique dining experience, and to respect and honor 3 different cultures by sharing their culinary traditions with our customers.
